# Mia — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-24_

This privacy policy explains how the Mia app ("the app", "we") handles your information. Mia is a Georgian-language voice AI assistant.

## 1. What we collect

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Microphone audio** | Only while you're actively talking to Mia (tap the orb to start, stops automatically when you stop speaking) | To transcribe your speech into text so Mia can understand and reply |
| **Approximate location** | Only when you ask a location-dependent question (e.g. weather) and have granted permission | To return weather/time relevant to your area |
| **Account info (name, email)** | When you register | To personalise your experience and let you sign in across devices |
| **Conversation history** | Stored on your device | So you can revisit past conversations, and so Mia can follow the current one |
| **Personal memory** (facts you tell Mia, e.g. name, city) | Stored on your device; reviewable and deletable in Settings | So Mia remembers you across conversations |

We do **not** collect: contacts, photos, calendar, files, precise location, advertising IDs, sensor data, or any data you didn't explicitly provide.

## 2. Third-party processors

To deliver the voice AI experience, we send specific pieces of data to these services:

| Service | What we send | Their privacy policy |
|---|---|---|
| Google Gemini API | Your transcribed speech + recent conversation context | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| ElevenLabs (Flash v3 TTS) | Mia's reply text, to synthesise audio | https://elevenlabs.io/privacy |
| Camb.ai (optional Georgian TTS) | Mia's reply text, only if you select Camb in settings | https://www.camb.ai/privacy-policy |
| Google Cloud (optional STT) | Your audio, only if Google STT mode is enabled | https://policies.google.com/privacy |
| OpenStreetMap Nominatim (reverse geocoding) | Your approximate coordinates | https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy |
| ipwho.is (IP geolocation) | Your IP address, only when you ask about weather and your phone shares no location | https://ipwhois.io/privacy |

These processors act on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy commitments.

## 3. Data retention

- **Audio recordings**: not stored. Audio is transcribed in transit and the recording is discarded immediately.
- **Transcripts and conversations**: stored locally on your device. You can delete any conversation from the in-app conversations drawer.
- **Account info**: stored on our server until you delete your account.
- **Location**: cached locally for ~10 minutes to avoid repeated geocoding lookups.

## 4. Your rights

- **Delete a conversation** — open the conversations drawer, tap the trash icon next to it.
- **Delete your account** — open Settings → tap "გასვლა" (sign out), then email us to request full account deletion.
- **Revoke permissions** — at any time via your phone's Settings → Apps → Mia → Permissions.

## 5. Children

Mia is not intended for users under 13.

## 6. Security

- All network traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).
- API keys for third-party services are held on our server, never embedded in the app.
- Account passwords are hashed before storage.

## 7. Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
